About me

A bit about who I am

Hi — I’m Raymond. I’m a data science student at the Eastern Africa Statistical Training Centre, and I also build software for day-to-day business and public-sector work.

Most of my projects start with a practical question: Can we score SME credit risk more fairly? Can a hospital track patients and pharmacy stock without chaos? Can tax field work leave cleaner, more useful records?

I like staying close to the full path — collecting and cleaning data, testing models, then putting results into a simple app or dashboard. Outside class and client work, I coach students in computing and statistics.
