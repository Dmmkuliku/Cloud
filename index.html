Stewardship

Data ethics & security as design requirements

Models affect livelihoods and care pathways. I bake privacy, fairness, and security into the architecture — not as an afterthought slide.

01

Purpose limitation

Collect and process only what the decision requires. Document lawful purpose, retention, and deletion paths before production use.

02

Fairness & bias vigilance

Especially in credit and health contexts: inspect disparate impact, avoid proxy discrimination, and prefer transparent score explanations.

03

Privacy by design

Minimize identifiers, protect PII in logs, encrypt in transit, and apply role-based access so sensitive records stay compartmentalized.

04

Secure system posture

Input validation, constrained CORS, security headers (CSP, HSTS, frame denial), session hygiene, and least-privilege service accounts.

05

Accountability & consent

Clear stakeholder communication, informed use of data subjects’ information, and audit trails that make automated decisions reviewable.

06

Responsible deployment

Human oversight for high-stakes outputs, monitoring for drift, and rollback plans when models behave outside expected envelopes.
