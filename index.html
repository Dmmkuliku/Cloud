How I work

A practical data science workflow

From question to insight to working system — useful for partners, clients, and reviewers of my work.

  1. 01

    Frame the question

    Clarify the decision, the metrics that matter, and the data that can answer it.

  2. 02

    Extract & clean

    Pull data from records and databases, fix quality issues, and keep entities consistent.

  3. 03

    Model & validate

    Apply statistical and predictive methods — regression, ordinal logistic, and related analytics.

  4. 04

    Communicate

    Translate findings into reports, dashboards, and recommendations stakeholders can use.

  5. 05

    Ship securely

    Put results into applications and cloud services with careful handling of sensitive data.
